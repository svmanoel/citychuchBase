OUR FOUNDATION

Faith that shapes our lives

At City Church Liverpool, our faith is centred on Jesus Christ and the message of the Bible.

We believe that God has called us to love Him, love one another and share His love with the world around us.

Our beliefs provide the foundation for how we worship, how we serve and how we live together as a church family.

God

We believe in one God who exists eternally as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

God is loving, holy, just and faithful. He created us and desires a relationship with every person.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's Word and is the foundation of our faith and the way we live.

Through Scripture we discover God's character, His purposes and His plan for humanity.

Jesus Christ

We believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God and the centre of our faith.

He came into the world to reveal God's love, lived a sinless life, died for our sins and rose again from the dead.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is present and active in the world today.

He guides, strengthens and empowers believers to live lives that honour God and serve others.

Salvation

We believe salvation is a gift from God through faith in Jesus Christ.

Through Jesus we can experience forgiveness, new life and a restored relationship with God.

The Church

We believe the Church is a family of people who follow Jesus Christ.

We are called to worship together, care for one another, serve our communities and share the good news of Jesus.

Baptism

We believe baptism is an important expression of faith and a public declaration of our decision to follow Jesus.

It represents new life and our identification with Christ.

Our Mission

We believe the Church is called to share the Gospel and demonstrate God's love through practical action.

We want to be a church that serves Liverpool, supports those around us and helps people discover life with Jesus.
