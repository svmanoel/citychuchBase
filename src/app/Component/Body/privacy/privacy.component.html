PRIVACY

Your privacy matters to us.

City Church Liverpool is committed to protecting your personal information and handling it responsibly, transparently and in accordance with UK data protection law.

Introduction

At City Church Liverpool, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we keep it safe and the rights you have in relation to your personal information.

What Information Do We Collect?

We only collect information that you choose to share with us, such as when you:

  • Complete a contact form.
  • Register through ChurchSuite or MyChurchSuite.
  • Sign up to receive emails or newsletters.
  • Enquire about an event or ministry.

This may include your name, email address, telephone number and other information relevant to helping us support and communicate with you.

How Do We Use Your Information?

We use your information to:

  • Keep you informed about church life and events.
  • Provide pastoral care and support.
  • Help you connect with groups, teams and ministries.
  • Manage church administration through ChurchSuite.
  • Meet our legal and regulatory responsibilities.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

· CHURCH ADMINISTRATION

ChurchSuite and MyChurchSuite

Some church administration and communication activities may be managed through ChurchSuite or MyChurchSuite. Information provided through these services is handled in accordance with the relevant service's privacy and security arrangements.

If you register with us through ChurchSuite or MyChurchSuite, you may be asked to provide additional information relevant to your involvement with City Church.

Keeping Your Information Safe

Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to authorised church leaders and administrators who need it for legitimate church purposes.

We regularly review our processes and take appropriate steps to help ensure that your personal information remains protected from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers where this is necessary to operate church services, manage administration, communicate with you or meet our legal obligations.

Where information is shared with a service provider, we take reasonable steps to ensure that your information is handled appropriately and securely.

You have a say in how your information is used.

If you have questions or concerns about your personal information, please contact us.

Depending on the circumstances, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request that we delete your information where appropriate.
  • Update your communication preferences at any time.

Communication Preferences

If you receive communications from City Church and no longer wish to receive them, you can contact us to update your preferences.

We will respect your communication preferences and will only continue to contact you where we have an appropriate reason or legal basis to do so.

External Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites and services, including services used by City Church for administration, communication or events.

Once you leave our website, the privacy practices of the external website or service will apply.
