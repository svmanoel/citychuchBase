OUR VISION

Jesus transforms lives.

Through His love, He draws us into relationship with God, shapes us to become more like Him, places us in authentic community, and sends us out to share His hope with the world.

That’s why we’re passionate about helping people know Jesus, grow together and live on mission.

OUR MISSION

We believe the good news of Jesus is not something we keep to ourselves. We are called to live it out in our homes, workplaces, friendships and communities, bringing hope wherever God has placed us.
