HOME GROUPS

Growing together.

Our Home Groups are a great way to build friendships, explore the Bible and pray together.

Home Groups

Home Groups meet every other week across Liverpool. They are a place where we can build genuine friendships, explore the Bible together, pray and encourage one another.

Whether you've been part of church for years or you're just starting to explore faith, Home Groups are a place where you can ask questions, grow and do life together.

FIND YOUR PLACE

ALPHA

Explore faith.

Alpha is a relaxed and welcoming space where you can explore the Christian faith, ask honest questions and have conversations without pressure.

Alpha

Alpha provides a relaxed and welcoming space to explore Christian faith, ask honest questions and discover more about Jesus.

Each gathering includes time to connect with others, a short talk and an opportunity for open conversation.

You don't need any previous knowledge of the Bible or Christian faith. Come along, meet people and ask whatever questions you have.

CHILDREN & YOUNG PEOPLE

A place for every generation.

We want children and young people to experience a safe, welcoming and engaging environment where they can learn about Jesus and grow in faith.

Children & Young People

Creche

For children aged 0–4, providing a safe and welcoming environment for our youngest children.

Children

For children aged 5–10, with engaging environments designed to help them learn and grow.

Youth

For young people aged 11–16, creating space to explore faith, build friendships and grow together.

Our volunteers are safely recruited, DBS checked and trained to help create safe and welcoming environments for children and young people.

YOUNG ADULTS

Life, faith and what's next.

A place for young adults to build friendships, grow in Jesus and navigate university, careers and what's next.

Young Adults

Our Young Adults community is for people aged 16–26, whether you're at university, starting a career or figuring out what's next.

We meet regularly to build friendships, grow in our relationship with Jesus and spend time together.

Alongside our regular gatherings, there are opportunities for socials and spending time together outside of church.

SERVING

Everyone has a part to play.

We believe everyone has something to contribute. Serving is a great way to discover your gifts, meet people and find your place in the church family.

Serving at City Church

There are lots of different ways to get involved in church life. Whether you enjoy welcoming people, helping with children, serving with worship or supporting the practical needs of church, there is a place for you.

Serving isn't simply about filling a role. It's about using what God has given us to bless others and play our part in the life of the church.
