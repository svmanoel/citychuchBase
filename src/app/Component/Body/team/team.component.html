OUR TEAM

Meet the people behind City Church.

Meet the people, but more importantly, discover the heart behind City Church.

Behind every ministry, Sunday service and community event is a team of people who love Jesus and are passionate about serving others.

SENIOR PASTOR

James Earnshaw

James has been part of City Church for over a decade and has served as Senior Pastor since 2022.

He is passionate about helping people encounter Jesus, grow as disciples and discover the purpose God has for their lives.

Whether he’s preaching on a Sunday, leading the church or chatting over a coffee, James loves seeing people take their next step in following Jesus.

James is married to Cathy, and together they have two daughters, Alice and Eve.

Outside church you’ll often find him travelling, enjoying a barbecue, tackling another DIY project or planning the church’s next adventure.

CHILDREN'S LEADER

Cathy Earnshaw

Cathy leads our Children’s Ministry and has a genuine shepherd’s heart.

She is passionate about helping children discover Jesus in ways they can understand, enjoy and remember, while also encouraging and supporting families as they grow in faith together.

She also enjoys preaching and has a gift for helping people connect with God’s Word in a warm, practical and encouraging way.

Her kindness, wisdom and care are felt throughout the life of City Church.

Away from church, you’ll usually find Cathy with an Agatha Christie novel in hand or enjoying another episode of Friends with a cup of tea.

ORGANISER & PROBLEM SOLVER

Ben

Ben is City Church’s organiser and resident problem solver. He has an incredible ability to turn ideas into reality.

Whether it’s installing new technology, fixing something behind the scenes or making sure everything runs smoothly, Ben helps make things happen.

Alongside his practical gifts, Ben leads our Prayer Ministry and Young Adults with humility, wisdom and a real heart for people.

He’s also a gifted Bible teacher who loves helping others grow in their relationship with Jesus.

HOME GROUPS & MIDWEEK MINISTRIES

Jane

Jane oversees our Home Groups and many of our midweek ministries, helping people find community and grow in their faith.

She has a real gift for teaching and a shepherd’s heart for people, always taking time to encourage, listen and care for others.

Jane also brings organisation, wisdom and structure to the life of the church, quietly helping countless ministries flourish behind the scenes.

Her faithful leadership, steady encouragement and servant-hearted approach make her an invaluable part of the City Church family.
