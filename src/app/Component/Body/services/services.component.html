What to Expect

Every Sunday at 11:00am, we gather to worship God, learn from the Bible, pray together and enjoy being church family.

Contemporary Worship

Our worship is contemporary, heartfelt and focused on God. We believe worship is more than singing—it’s responding to who God is and what He has done.

Bible Teaching

Each week we teach from the Bible in a way that is practical, relevant and rooted in Scripture, helping us grow as followers of Jesus in everyday life.

Prayer & Reflection

There are opportunities throughout the service for prayer and reflection as we respond to God and seek His presence.

Children & Young People

We provide a safe and engaging programme for children and young people during the service.

Tea & Coffee

After the service there is plenty of time to chat, enjoy a tea or coffee and get to know one another.

New Here?

There’s no dress code, no pressure and no expectation that you’ll know what to do. Come as you are and join in as much or as little as you feel comfortable.

PRAYER

Prayer is at the heart of City Church.

We believe God hears prayer, speaks today and continues to transform lives.

Alongside praying in every Sunday service, we gather regularly throughout the month to seek God together.

Ways We Pray Together

I See a Cloud Prayer

Every other Tuesday | 7:00–8:00am

A focused hour of prayer as we seek God for our church, our city and all that He wants to do in the weeks ahead.

We gather with faith, believing God is always at work, even when we can’t yet see it.

Boiler Room Prayer

Every Sunday | 10:00–10:30am

Before every Sunday service we meet to pray for everyone who will be attending, everyone serving, and all that God wants to do that morning.

Everyone is welcome.

Prayer & Fasting

Once each month

Once a month we set aside a day to go deeper in seeking God through prayer and fasting.

Together we worship, pray, listen and make space for God to renew our hearts, strengthen our faith and guide us as a church.

Whether you’ve been praying for years or you’re just beginning, we’d love you to join us as we seek God together.
