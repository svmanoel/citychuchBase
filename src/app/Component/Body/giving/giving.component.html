Ways to Give

City Church wouldn’t exist without the generosity and faithfulness of so many.

If you’d like to partner with us in reaching our city and region with the love and message of Jesus Christ, there are several ways you can support our church.

Choose the method that works best for you.

Cash

If you wish to give using cash, you can place it in the offering basket on Sunday mornings when we meet together at church.

May we suggest that you put some money to one side each week, ready to give.

If you're signed up to Gift Aid, please continue placing your cash or cheques in your numbered envelopes.

Gift Aid

If you are a UK taxpayer, you can increase your giving to City Church through Gift Aid.

This enables us to claim back 25p of tax for every £1 you give, at no extra cost to you.

Simply complete a one-off declaration using our online form, or ask our treasurer for a paper copy.
